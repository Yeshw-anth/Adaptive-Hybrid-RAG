# The Solar System

Our solar system consists of the Sun and everything that orbits it, including eight planets and their moons, dwarf planets, asteroids, and comets. The Sun is a star that provides light and heat to the planets.

## The Planets

The eight planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. Earth is the third planet from the Sun and is the only known planet to support life.
